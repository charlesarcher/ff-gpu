Why it lost despite FREE occupancy (+4 waves AMD):
1. Production MR calls are overwhelmingly COMPOSITE and are decided by the
   FIRST witness (base 2 fails fast). Pairing forces chain B (base 3) to
   complete anyway — pure added latency on the dominant path. The baseline's
   single-witness early-return was load-bearing for the composite case.
2. NV paid an occupancy rung on top: 78 -> 90 regs drops resident blocks/SM
   3 -> 2 (~-33% threads/SM), compounding the extra work (+25.8%).
3. The ILP win (two independent mul-high chains per iteration) is real but
   only pays on PRIME n (full witness sweep); primes are rare among MR calls,
   and even there the trailing-squaring phase serialized on flags.
